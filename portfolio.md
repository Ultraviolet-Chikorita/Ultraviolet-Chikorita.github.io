## Research and selected projects

### Private Is Not Privileged

Mechanistic interpretability research testing whether activation-based predictors have a genuine information advantage over matched external baselines. In a 40-game Diplomacy experiment, an apparent white-box advantage reversed when the text observer received matched post-deliberation information.

- [Research page](https://apartresearch.com/project/private-is-not-privileged-18om)
- [Repository](https://github.com/Ultraviolet-Chikorita/Private-is-not-Privileged)
- [Paper](https://ultraviolet-chikorita.github.io/assets/papers/private-is-not-privileged.pdf)

### GraftLoyal

Mechanistically constructs context-sensitive secret loyalty in a frozen language model by reading internal representations and intervening through a gated writer vector. Evaluated on 1,152 prompts across counterfactual pairs and held-out domains.

- [Research page](https://apartresearch.com/project/graftloyal-a219)
- [Repository](https://github.com/Ultraviolet-Chikorita/graftloyal)
- [Paper](https://ultraviolet-chikorita.github.io/assets/papers/graftloyal.pdf)

### RiskLab

A multi-evaluator framework for detecting manipulative language-model behaviour using rule-based analysis, classifiers, and councils of judges while preserving provenance.

- [Research page](https://apartresearch.com/project/risklab-w0fy)
- [Repository](https://github.com/Ultraviolet-Chikorita/RiskLab)
- [Paper](https://ultraviolet-chikorita.github.io/assets/papers/risklab.pdf)
